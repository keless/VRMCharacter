## Animations

### Mixamo Animations

Most animations in this project were downloaded from [Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com/), an Adobe service that provides free motion-capture animations for 3D characters.

- **Source**: [Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com/)
- **Provider**: Adobe Inc.
- **License**: Free to use under Adobe's Terms of Use. Animations may be used in personal and commercial projects. They may not be redistributed or resold as standalone animation assets.
- **Reference**: [Adobe Mixamo Terms](https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-collective/online-membership/terms.html)

### VRMA Animations

Some animations are in VRMA format (VRM Animation), a specialized animation format for VRM models.

- **Source**: [VRMA Motion Pack](https://github.com/pixiv/three-vrm/tree/main/packages/three-vrm-animation/example/sample-motion)
- **Provider**: [pixiv/three-vrm](https://github.com/pixiv/three-vrm) on GitHub
- **License**: [MIT License](https://github.com/pixiv/three-vrm/blob/main/packages/three-vrm-animation/LICENSE)
